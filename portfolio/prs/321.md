- `webdriver`/`webdriverOwn`(스텔스 override 적용?), `uaHeadless`, `hasChrome`/`chromeRuntime`, `plugins`/`mimeTypes`/`languages`, `permMismatch`, `glVendor`/`glRenderer`(headless 텔).
